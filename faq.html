Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

How do I book a session?

Simply visit our Contact page, send us a message, or call us. We'll confirm your date after receiving your booking deposit.

How much is the booking deposit?

A 50% deposit secures your booking. The remaining balance is due before the final delivery.

How long does photo delivery take?

Portrait sessions are typically delivered within 5–7 working days, while weddings and large events may take 2–4 weeks depending on the package.

Do you travel outside your city?

Yes. We are available to travel nationwide. Travel and accommodation costs may apply depending on the location.

Do you provide raw photos?

No. We deliver professionally selected and edited images to ensure every photograph meets our quality standards.

Can I customize a package?

Absolutely. We can tailor a package to suit your event, budget, and specific requirements.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer and other approved payment methods will be provided during the booking process.

What happens if my event runs longer?

Additional coverage can be arranged at an agreed hourly rate if our schedule permits.
